Roomb-O-Matic

Capture the robot password on the local network. This works in Docker without a terminal.

Before you press the button

  1. Close the iRobot app. The robot allows only one local connection.
  2. For an i-series robot (i5 and similar), press and hold the Home button — the dock / house icon — for about 2 seconds, until you hear tones.
  3. Do not hold both buttons. That is the 900-series gesture.
  4. Leave this page open. Capture retries until the robot offers a password.
